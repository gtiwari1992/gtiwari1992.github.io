About

I am a Senior Data Analyst in Cardiovascular Research at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston, where I lead computational analyses of genomic, proteomic and metabolomic data from epidemiological and clinical cohorts. My work spans genome-wide and phenome-wide association studies, polygenic risk scores optimised for diverse populations, and integrative multi-omic models that link molecular measurements to longitudinal clinical outcomes.

Findings from this work have appeared in Nature Metabolism, Circulation, Diabetes Care, JACC: Basic to Translational Science and other flagship journals of the American Heart Association. I collaborate closely with cardiologists, epidemiologists and translational scientists, mentor doctoral and postdoctoral researchers on methodology, and contribute to federal grant applications and consortium-level research strategy.

Before moving into biomedical research I worked in financial data science at ICICI Bank and in software engineering at Accenture, and I hold graduate degrees in data analytics and finance alongside an engineering undergraduate degree.
